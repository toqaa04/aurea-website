Audit

Annual & consolidated financial statement audits

Audits in accordance with the German Commercial Code (HGB) and International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) – thorough, efficient and risk-based.

Special audits & certifications

Audits relating to the formation, conversion or merger of companies, as well as other special audits (e.g. pursuant to section 53 of the German Company Formation Act).

Internal control systems & compliance audits

Analysis and improvement of internal processes and control mechanisms – to minimise risk and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements.

Accounting advisory & business consulting

Support with complex accounting issues and analysis of business processes.

Energy audits

Audits in accordance with, amongst others, the Renewable Energy Sources Act (EEG), the Combined Heat and Power Act (KWKG) or the Energy Financing Act (EnFG).

ESG reporting & sustainability strategy

Audit and support for the implementation of ESG-compliant reporting in accordance with CSRD, VSME, GRI or the EU Taxonomy.

Tax

Ongoing tax advice

Preparation of annual accounts and income statements in accordance with commercial and tax law. Preparation of personal and business tax returns. Assistance with tax audits and out-of-court appeal proceedings.

Structuring consultancy

Advice on setting up a business, choosing a legal form, reorganisation and tax structuring of corporate groups.

Financial accounting

Digital and transparent financial accounting with secure electronic document exchange. Submission of advance VAT returns. Provision of business analytics, as well as the creation and maintenance of fixed asset records.

Payroll accounting

Reliable monthly payroll processing, including income tax returns, contribution statements, certificates, and support during audits by social security authorities and tax offices.

Tax planning & structuring advice

Advice on the exercise of accounting and valuation options, as well as the preparation of tax opinions. Development of sound tax strategies.

Succession & wealth planning

Tailored planning for business and asset succession, including valuations for inheritance and gift tax purposes, as well as tax-optimised transfer strategies.

Planning & investment appraisal

Well-founded tax planning and investment analyses, support with financing enquiries, and advice on all aspects of property investment.

Transaction & acquisition advisory

Tax advice on company acquisitions and disposals – from due diligence and transaction structuring to support with contract negotiations.
